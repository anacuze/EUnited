Community rules

EUnited is a place for students of the EUPeace universities. Everyone who comments has signed up with a university email address, and comments appear under your name.

What we ask

  • Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people. No insults, harassment or threats.
  • No hate. Nothing that attacks people for their nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation or disability.
  • Stay on topic. Comment on the post. No advertising and no spam.
  • Respect privacy. Don't share other people's personal details, photos or private messages.
  • Nothing illegal.

How comments are checked

  • A comment appears as soon as you post it. A few simple rules apply automatically: a short wait between two comments, at most {{ max_links }} links, and no repeating the same comment.
  • A comment that contains a word from our list of blocked words{% if automatic_check %}, or that an automatic check flags as possibly abusive,{% endif %} is held back until a moderator has read it. You still see it, marked as waiting.
    • {% if automatic_check %}
  • The automatic check is done by an outside service (OpenAI's Moderation API), which receives the text of the comment but not your name or email address.
    • {% endif %}
  • Anyone logged in can report a comment. Moderators read every report, and a comment reported by several students is taken down until a moderator has decided.
  • Moderators can hide a comment that breaks these rules. A person, not a program, makes that decision.

If you disagree

If your comment was held back or hidden and you think that was a mistake, write to the EUnited team and a moderator will look at it again. You can delete your own comments at any time, and they are deleted with your account.
